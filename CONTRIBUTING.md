# How doctrine changes

No body may certify the correctness of its own work.

1. **Proposal.** Anyone opens a pull request against the Canon. Say what changes and why.
2. **Witness.** A Witness is assigned to challenge it. The proposer never picks their own Witness.
3. **Debate.** 14 days for Articles. 7 for everything else.
4. **Evidence.** The proposer answers with evidence, not authority. Titles carry no weight here, including the Founder's.
5. **Adoption.** The Canon Council merges. The version bumps and the change is recorded in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## Versioning

- **Major** (v2.0.0): an Article is added, removed, or its tenet changes.
- **Minor** (v1.1.0): an Observation or Warning is added, removed, or changes status.
- **Patch** (v1.0.1): commentary, clarity, typos.

## Ground rules

- Challenge the claim, not the person.
- Steelman first. State the claim better than its author did, then break it.
- A Witness who changes their mind has done the job well.
- The Archive cannot be private. Debates happen in the open, on the pull request.
