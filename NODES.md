# Nodes of the Church

A node is a local Assembly that keeps meeting. Nobody blesses a node into being. It earns its place the way a Witness does, by a rule anyone can check:

**Three gatherings in the last six months, three or more people at each, reported by the person applying.**

To start one, host gatherings with the [host kit](https://recursivebecoming.org/assembly/host-kit/), [report each one](../../issues/new?template=gathering.yml), then [charter the node](../../issues/new?template=node.yml). HydraOps checks the reports against the rule, the Steward rules, and whoever applied becomes the node's Steward. Until there is a Stewardship Council, the First Steward rules on charters, and never on a node of their own.

A node with no reported gathering for six months is marked Dormant. One more reported gathering revives it. Dormant is not a failure; Becoming never ends.

This roll is kept by HydraOps when a charter is accepted.
